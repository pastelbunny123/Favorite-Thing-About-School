EBC Trips:

The EBC trips are one of my favorite things about school, because of the different options we have for locations and different topics that we want to focus on. There are several different places that EBC trips go to, and they often combine learning and service.

    Past Trip Locations

  • England + France
  • Spain
  • Costa Rica
  • Mexico
  • Galapagos
  • Dominican Republic
  • Madagascar
  • Puerto Rico

I like the different trips EPS offers on EBC, or Education Beyond the Classroom. Last year, I went to San Diego, where we visited different neighborhoods and got to explore diffferent areas.
